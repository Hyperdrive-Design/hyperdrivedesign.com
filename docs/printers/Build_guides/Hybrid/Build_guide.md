# Galaxy One Hybrid Build Guide

Build the Galaxy One Hybrid with illustrated, step-by-step assembly instructions. New parts and hardware appear in **orange**; the existing assembly appears in gray. Detailed steps show the assembly before installation, the parts being added, and the result.

This revision documents the CAD assembly and remains subject to physical build review. Unconfirmed fastener assignments, panel fixings, and retention details are marked on the relevant pages. Wiring, grounding, electrical ratings, firmware, toolhead fitment, and commissioning require the confirmations recorded in the guide.
