## Parts and tools

Gather these parts for both sides.

- 2 updated printed belt ties sized for your belts. [6 mm STL](https://github.com/Hyperdrive-Design/Hypernova/blob/main/STL/belt_ties/6mm_belt_tie_X2.stl?raw=true) or [9 mm STL](https://github.com/Hyperdrive-Design/Hypernova/blob/main/STL/belt_ties/9mm_belt_tie_X2.stl?raw=true)
- 2 × 25 mm belt-tie spacers
- 2 × M3 × 8 countersunk Torx screws
- 2 × MakerBeam XL T-nuts

Use a Torx driver. Power off the printer and release belt tension before removing the old ties. Keep enough belt length for the new loops. Build one clamp for each side.

## 1. Fold the belt end

**Action**

Insert the belt end into the belt tie. Fold the belt back with the teeth facing each other.

**Check**

Match the loop orientation shown.

![Fold the belt end](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-01.png)

## 2. Position the belt tie

**Action**

Slide the belt tie over the folded belt loop.

**Check**

Keep the folded sections aligned.

![Position the belt tie](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-02.png)

## 3. Insert the spacer

**Hardware**

25 mm belt-tie spacer
Use one per belt tie.

**Action**

Slide the spacer through the belt loop.

**Check**

Center it in the loop. This is a different part from the Beacon probe standoffs.

![Insert the spacer](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-03.png)

## 4. Center the spacer

**Action**

Center the spacer, then pull the belts tight around it.

**Check**

Keep the folded belt sections aligned.

![Center the spacer](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-04.png)

## 5. Insert the clamp screw

**Hardware**

M3 × 8 countersunk Torx screw

**Action**

Insert the screw from the side shown.

**Check**

Keep the belt tie and spacer aligned.

![Insert the clamp screw](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-05.png)

## 6. Check screw position

**Check**

Compare the screw position with this view before fitting the T-nut.
Keep the belt loop seated around the spacer.

![Check screw position](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-06.png)

## 7. Fit the MakerBeam XL T-nut

**Hardware**

MakerBeam XL T-nut
Use one per belt tie.

**Action**

Fit the T-nut in the orientation shown.

**Check**

Keep the screw and T-nut aligned.

![Fit the MakerBeam XL T-nut](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-07.png)

## 8. Tighten and check the T-nut

**Action**

Tighten the clamp screw with a Torx driver.

**Check**

Compare the T-nut position with this completed view.
Keep the belt tie, screw and T-nut aligned.

![Tighten and check the T-nut](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-08.png)

## 9. Completed clamp check

**Check**

Compare the assembled clamp with this view.
Confirm the belt loop, spacer and hardware are in their illustrated positions.

![Completed clamp check](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-09.png)

## 10. Position the first clamp

**Action**

Position the first belt clamp on the toolhead as shown.

**Check**

Align the clamp with the top screw path.

![Position the first clamp](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-10.png)

## 11. Install the first top screw

**Hardware**

1 × M3 × 40 countersunk Torx screw

**Action**

Hold the belt tie in place and insert the screw.

**Check**

Keep the clamp aligned.

![Install the first top screw](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-11.png)

## 12. Secure the first clamp

**Action**

Hold the belt tie in place while tightening the M3 × 40 countersunk Torx screw.

**Check**

Keep the clamp seated in the illustrated position.

![Secure the first clamp](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-12.png)

## 13. Position the opposite clamp

**Action**

Position the opposite belt clamp as shown.

**Check**

Align it with the remaining top screw path.

![Position the opposite clamp](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-13.png)

## 14. Install the opposite top screw

**Hardware**

1 × M3 × 40 countersunk Torx screw

**Action**

Hold the belt tie in place and insert the screw.

**Check**

Keep the clamp aligned.

![Install the opposite top screw](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-14.png)

## 15. Secure the opposite clamp

**Action**

Hold the belt tie in place while tightening the M3 × 40 countersunk Torx screw.

**Check**

Keep the clamp seated in the illustrated position.

![Secure the opposite clamp](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-15.png)

## 16. Inspect both sides

**Check**

Compare both belt clamps with the completed view.
Check that the belts are untwisted and the clamps are seated.
Retension the belts per your printer’s procedure.

![Inspect both sides](/assets/products/hypernova/belt_tie_upgrade/step-16.png)
